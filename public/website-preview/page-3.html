Before you begin

Plan your business website

What is included in a website project?

The written scope identifies the pages, content, design, forms, integrations, launch work, and handoff. A business website may include service pages, project examples, contact paths, mobile layouts, and search foundations. Booking systems, payments, dashboards, and ongoing maintenance are scoped separately when needed.

Can you redesign an existing website without losing its useful content?

A redesign begins by reviewing existing pages, URLs, content, and customer actions. Useful material can be retained and reorganized. If URLs change, redirect planning helps visitors and search engines reach the replacement pages. Domain and hosting changes are coordinated as part of the agreed launch scope.

Can the website connect to booking, ordering, or other business tools?

Booking, ordering, payment, and lead-capture connections can be included when the selected platform supports them. Before quoting an integration, we review its documentation, account access, subscription requirements, and the information it needs to exchange. Third-party fees and permissions are confirmed before development.

What should I prepare before requesting a website quote?

Share your existing website, services, audience, service area, brand assets, approved photos, desired pages, and the action you want customers to take. Mention any booking, payment, or management tools already in use. The pricing calculator offers a planning estimate; the final proposal defines the deliverables and cost.
