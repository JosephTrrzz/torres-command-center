Before you begin

Understand your local search project

What is the difference between website SEO and Google Business Profile work?

Website SEO covers page content, headings, internal links, metadata, crawl access, canonical URLs, and measurement. Google Business Profile work addresses the business information and service details shown on that profile. Both should accurately describe the same business, but they are managed separately.

Does submitting a sitemap mean my pages are indexed?

No. A sitemap helps search engines discover URLs. Submission, crawling, indexing, and appearing for a search are separate steps. Search Console can report whether a page is indexed and which canonical Google selected. No sitemap submission or indexing request guarantees inclusion or a ranking.

Does every city need its own page?

A location page is useful when it contains distinct information about real services, projects, or customer needs in that place. Repeating a page with only the city name changed offers little value. We begin with accurate service coverage and useful service pages, then identify where a dedicated location page would help customers.

How is SEO progress measured?

Reporting can review Search Console impressions, clicks, queries, and indexed pages alongside analytics and configured contact events. Baselines and reporting access are agreed at the start. Results are described using observed data, with priorities adjusted as evidence becomes available.
