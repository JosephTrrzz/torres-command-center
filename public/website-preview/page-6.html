The assignment

Present one distinctive residence with the depth and atmosphere of a dedicated luxury publication, while keeping the property information clear and discoverable.

The approach

Built a multi-page experience around the residence, setting, Pacific Northwest lifestyle, relocation information, and private-tour path.

Used the property’s real photography and restrained editorial design to let the home—not interface decoration—lead the story.

Implemented unique titles and descriptions, canonical URLs, indexable pages, structured metadata, and an image-aware XML sitemap.
